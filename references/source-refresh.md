# Refreshing current training practice

Use this process when the user asks for the latest evidence, the current coaching trend, or cases from online platforms.

## Source roles

1. Primary/authoritative: PubMed, journal pages, consensus statements, federation or platform documentation.
2. Applied practice: named coaches, team staff, athlete interviews, coach surveys, detailed case studies.
3. Community signal: YouTube, Bilibili, Reddit, X, Xiaohongshu, podcasts, forums.

Never promote a community trend to a prescription unless stronger evidence supports it. Record publication date separately from crawl date.

## Query matrix

Search both Chinese and English with the current year and previous year. Combine the sport with:

- durability, fatigue resistance, power after kJ, decoupling;
- FTP/critical power, absolute power, power-to-weight, W′/FRC, P-max, sprint and repeated sprint;
- training intensity distribution, polarized, pyramidal, sweet spot, threshold;
- VO2 interval duration, micro-intervals, time at VO2max;
- strength training, low cadence, torque;
- carbohydrate per hour, gut training, hydration, heat acclimation;
- recovery, HRV, sleep, subjective wellness, RED-S;
- amateur case study, masters, female cyclist, time-crunched cyclist;
- Intervals.icu API, workout builder syntax, device sync.

Chinese examples: `骑行 耐久性 疲劳后功率`, `自行车 极化 金字塔 甜区 训练`, `骑行 每小时碳水 肠胃训练`, `骑行 功率训练 案例`.

Prioritize sport-science journals and sections that publish relevant work, including *Sports Medicine*, *International Journal of Sports Physiology and Performance*, *Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise*, *European Journal of Applied Physiology*, *Journal of Applied Physiology*, *Scandinavian Journal of Medicine & Science in Sports*, *Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research*, and *International Journal of Sport Nutrition and Exercise Metabolism*. Search the paper and its corrections or commentary, not only press coverage.

## Channel routing

- Use normal web search for journal literature, official product docs, news, and direct webpages.
- Use Agent Reach when available for Bilibili, YouTube, X, Reddit, Xiaohongshu, and podcast search/extraction. Run its health check only for the channels being used.
- If a login-dependent channel is unavailable, report the gap and continue with accessible channels. Do not substitute invented discussion.
- For long videos or podcasts, inspect metadata first, then fetch subtitles/transcript only for promising sources.

## Extract a claim card

For each important source capture:

- claim;
- source type and publication date;
- population and sample size;
- intervention or observed practice;
- outcome;
- important limitation;
- practical implication for this athlete;
- stable URL.

Prefer a few claim cards that change the plan over a long list of links.

## Case translation

Before using a case, follow [case-comparison.md](case-comparison.md). Prefer the athlete's own successful history, then matched controlled research, then detailed cases. Compare goal and test, baseline, weekly volume, training age, sex/age where relevant, climate, equipment, adherence, nutrition support, and recovery resources. Search for unsuccessful comparators, dropouts, and alternative explanations. Scale the stimulus to the user's recent tolerance. Preserve the training purpose, not the professional dose.

## Update the snapshot

When a new source materially changes a standing claim, update `training-evidence.md`, change its review date, and retain the boundary or limitation. Do not add a source merely because it is new.
